crOOZin

Parametric light object · Limited series · Paris

A biomorphic light sculpture born from Grasshopper-driven parametric design. Inspired by Art Nouveau fluidity and Zaha Hadid's structural logic, crOOZin is printed at 25 micron resolution in translucent resin — a form that comes alive only when light passes through it.

Each piece is unique. Each form is a conversation between code, material, and light.

Dimensions Ø 15 cm × 27 cm
Material 3D-printed resin · 25 µm · Hand-turned wood base
Light source LED 2700K dimmable · Dichroic lens · USB-C
Production Conçu & fabriqué à Paris · Série numérotée
Studio Studio Incandescence · 2026

Architectural Lighting — Residential

Villa Sedrati

Residential · Rabat, Morocco · 2025

A refined residential lighting scheme balancing high-end comfort with cost efficiency. The design integrates layered lighting: discreet DALI-controlled downlights for general illumination, continuous LED coves for ambient architectural highlights, and focused accents using rail-mounted spots and minimalist pendants to enhance artworks and spatial depth.

Premium fixtures by Arkoslight were selected for key living areas, while Hofflights and Luxio products ensured budget-conscious consistency in secondary spaces and the home cinema. The result is a custom, responsive lighting environment tailored to the villa's elegance and functionality.

Location Rabat, Morocco
Sector Residential · High-end
Client Luxio Lighting
Stage Consulting · DIALux
Date June 2025
Fixtures Arkoslight · Hofflights · Luxio
