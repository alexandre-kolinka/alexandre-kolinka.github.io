Obsession

Senior lighting designer and scenographer with over 15 years of international experience. From stadium skins in Saudi Arabia to cultural venues in Kazakhstan, from luxury retail in Paris to parametric light objects born in a Paris studio.

Founder of Studio Incandescence — working at the intersection of parametric design, LED electronics, and fabrication. Currently developing the crOOZin series: biomorphic light objects grown from Grasshopper geometries and printed at 25 micron resolution.

Fluent in Rhino · Grasshopper · DIALux · Madrix · LightAct · Cinema 4D · Arduino · DMX/ArtNet · Unreal Engine · V-Ray.
