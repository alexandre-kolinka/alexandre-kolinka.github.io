Senior lighting designer and scenographer with over 15 years of international experience across architectural lighting, media façades, and public realm installations. From stadium skins in Saudi Arabia to cultural venues in Kazakhstan, from luxury retail in Paris to parametric light objects born in a Paris studio.

Founder of Studio Incandescence — an independent research studio working at the intersection of parametric design, LED electronics, and fabrication. Currently developing the crOOZin series: biomorphic light objects grown from Grasshopper geometries and printed at 25 micron resolution.

Fluent in Rhino · Grasshopper · DIALux · Madrix · LightAct · Cinema 4D · Arduino · DMX/ArtNet · Unreal Engine · V-Ray.

crOOZin

Parametric light object · Limited series · Paris

A biomorphic light sculpture born from Grasshopper-driven parametric design. Inspired by Art Nouveau fluidity and Zaha Hadid's structural logic, crOOZin is printed at 25 micron resolution in translucent resin — a form that comes alive only when light passes through it.

Each piece is unique. Each form is a conversation between code, material, and light.

Dimensions Ø 15 cm × 27 cm
Material 3D-printed resin · 25 µm · Hand-turned wood base
Light source LED 2700K dimmable · Dichroic lens · USB-C
Production Conçu & fabriqué à Paris · Série numérotée
