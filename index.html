Dust Memory

Light · Sand · Laser · UAE · In development

"In the heart of Ras Al Khaimah's historic fishermen's village, Dust Memory materialises as a hovering cube of blue light, scaled just shy of the surrounding coral-stone houses."

A minimal dark-aluminium frame suspends a vibrating ceiling grid, releasing a whisper-thin veil of local sand that cascades through horizontal laser planes. Each grain becomes a momentary star, sparking as it intersects the beams and dissolving into darkness as it falls — an ever-changing constellation that fuses the site's maritime past with a vision of technological poetics.

Visitors are invited to walk the perimeter and witness tradition, light and gravity in quiet dialogue, framing the Biennale's wider conversation on memory and future craft.

Parametric Design
& Research

Where the hand gets lost, the code continues. Algorithmic tools for light distribution, generative form and fabrication logic — building environments where aesthetic intention and technical deployment are resolved in the same gesture.

Where the hand
gets lost

Parametric design is not a style — it is an extension of the hand, precisely where the hand gets lost. Where the pencil hesitates, the code continues. Using Rhino and Grasshopper, I build algorithms that generate form from rules: structural logic, light distribution, material behaviour, fabrication constraints.

But beyond form itself, parametric design is memory. Every iteration is recorded, every decision traceable. The form does not appear — it reveals itself progressively, through time and space, as a sequence of states each carrying the trace of the one before.

The crOOZin luminaires, the Dammam Stadium façade strategy, the Ligeo spatial distribution tool — all emerge from the same dialogue between intention and code, between the limits of the hand and the infinity of the rule.

Project 01 · 2021

Ligeo Spatial
Distribution

Grasshopper · Perlin Noise · ArtNet · sACN · Madrix

A parametric tool developed in February 2021 for Ligeo Lighting, designed to solve a precise spatial problem: how to distribute a series of LED bars in three-dimensional space with controlled randomness. The algorithm uses Perlin Noise — a smooth, organic generative model — to avoid mechanical regularity while maintaining full designer control over density, angular amplitude, bar length and spatial recursion within a defined volume.

Unlike discrete assembly approaches such as Wave Function Collapse — which fill a volume with pre-defined modules following connectivity rules — this tool generates a continuous, analogue distribution governed by physical constraints: bars ranging from 30 to 150 cm, angular ranges from 20° to 160°, angular shift from -45° to +45°, and hub points supporting up to 5 recursive branches. Each bar is assigned a unique IP address, auto-generated by the tool, ready for ArtNet/sACN deployment in Madrix and compatible light software.

The output also includes a hanging point schedule — coordinates and lengths — for on-site installation. A design environment where aesthetic intention, structural logic and technical deployment are resolved in the same gesture.

Architectural Lighting — Residential

Villa Sedrati

Residential · Rabat, Morocco · 2025

A refined residential lighting scheme balancing high-end comfort with cost efficiency. The design integrates layered lighting: discreet DALI-controlled downlights for general illumination, continuous LED coves for ambient architectural highlights, and focused accents using rail-mounted spots and minimalist pendants to enhance artworks and spatial depth.

Premium fixtures by Arkoslight were selected for key living areas, while Hofflights and Luxio products ensured budget-conscious consistency in secondary spaces and the home cinema. The result is a custom, responsive lighting environment tailored to the villa's elegance and functionality.

Location Rabat, Morocco
Sector Residential · High-end
Client Luxio Lighting
Stage Consulting · DIALux
